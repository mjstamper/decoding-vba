## Who this course is for

You completed the free Beginner and Intermediate tracks—or you maintain Excel files at work and need **project-level** examples, not isolated syntax demos.

## How it works

1. Work through projects in order (each builds on the last).
2. Open the provided `.xlsm`, read the PDF for that project, then watch the walkthrough.
3. Adapt the patterns to your own sheets using the included module layout.
